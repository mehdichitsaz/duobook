DuoBook Host for Mac

The free menu-bar app that lets DuoBook on your iPhone show your Mac’s screen and type and click on it.

Download for Mac Setup help

Version 1.3.4 · macOS 14 Sonoma or later · Apple silicon and Intel · Signed and notarized by Apple · Updates itself

Install

  1. Open the downloaded file and drag DuoBook Host to Applications.
  2. Open it from Applications. Its icon appears in the menu bar.
  3. Its setup window has an Allow button for Screen Recording and one for Accessibility; each opens the right page in System Settings.
  4. Turn on Open at login so it’s always ready.
  5. Want to use your Mac away from home? Turn on Access from anywhere.

New in 1.3.4

  • Full menu bar: when your menu bar has no room left for DuoBook Host’s icon (on a MacBook, macOS hides extra icons behind the camera), DuoBook Host now tells you and shows how to make room, instead of seeming not to open. Opening DuoBook Host again always shows its menu, in a window if the icon is hidden, and the icon comes back by itself once there’s room.

New in 1.3.3

  • Menu over full-screen apps: clicking DuoBook Host’s menu bar icon while an app is in full screen now opens the menu right there, on your Mac or from your iPhone, instead of only on the desktop. With more than one display, it opens on the display you clicked.

New in 1.3.2

  • Screen cover always locks first: if your Mac’s display turns off or its screen saver starts after your iPhone has left, the screen cover now locks your Mac for real before it comes down, so waking the Mac always asks for its password.

New in 1.3.1

  • Videos keep playing: when your Mac’s own display turns off or its screen saver starts while you use it from your iPhone, DuoBook Host no longer says the Mac is locked and freezes the picture. Your iPhone only shows the lock screen when your Mac really needs its password.
  • Away from home on more routers: DuoBook Host gets along with more routers’ UPnP, including ones that only allow the same port or a forward that never expires, and moves to the next port when another device already has DuoBook’s. When a second router or a shared internet address is in the way, it now says which, instead of just that away from home stopped working. What to do about each.

New in 1.3

  • DuoBook Anywhere: with the subscription on your iPhone, DuoBook Host meets it through DuoBook’s relay, so your iPhone reaches your Mac from any network, with nothing to set up. Still encrypted end to end: the relay can’t see your screen.
  • Open apps: tap Apps on your iPhone to open anything on your Mac, with your favorites first.
  • All your displays: on iPhone Duo, every display your Mac has shows live in a row above its screen. Tap one to switch.
  • Copy: copies what’s selected on your Mac straight to your iPhone.
  • Wakes your Mac: a sleeping Mac wakes up when you connect.
  • Permission help: if Accessibility is off, your Mac shows a notification that opens the right setting.
  • Your language: DuoBook Host now speaks French, Spanish, German, Italian, Portuguese, Japanese, Korean, and Chinese.
  • With DuoBook 1.3 on your iPhone: widgets, talk to type, and new glass controls.

New in 1.2

  • Cover your screen: while you use your Mac from your iPhone, its own screen can stay covered, so no one nearby sees it.
  • Big monitors: with Larger Text, a big display is readable on your iPhone, and zoom shows the Mac at full sharpness.
  • All Displays stays steady with three or more displays.
  • Mac questions: when your Mac shows something only the Mac can answer, your iPhone tells you instead of showing a black screen.
  • Away status: your iPhone says whether your Mac is ready to use away from home, or why it stopped working.
  • Move to Applications: opened from Downloads, DuoBook Host offers to move itself where it belongs.
  • With DuoBook 1.2 on your iPhone: 40 years of Apple looks, from today’s MacBook Pro back to the 1984 Macintosh.

New in 1.1

  • Unlock from iPhone: when your Mac is locked, your iPhone shows its lock screen, so you can type your password, even from far away. Connecting wakes a sleeping screen.
  • Updates itself: DuoBook Host offers new versions and installs them with one click, or choose Check for Updates… in its menu.
  • Easier setup: a window with an Allow button for each permission and a checkmark as each one turns on.
  • Keep Mac awake: on, the Mac doesn’t sleep by itself, plugged in or on battery, so your iPhone can reach it from anywhere. Keep the lid open: closing it still puts the Mac to sleep.
  • All Displays: with more than one display, see them all side by side on your iPhone.
  • Router help: if your router keeps DuoBook out when you’re away, DuoBook Host opens its settings and the guide.
  • Permissions that stick (1.1.1): if System Settings shows a permission as on but DuoBook Host still asks for it, DuoBook Host now clears the old switch when it opens, so Allow adds one that works.
  • Menu fix (1.1.2): on some Macs, DuoBook Host quit as soon as you clicked its menu bar icon. The menu now opens on every Mac.
  • With DuoBook 1.1 on your iPhone: the Touch Bar, up to 120 fps, a smooth pointer, and keys in your Mac’s language.

Why it needs these permissions

  • Screen Recording sends your screen to your iPhone, only while it’s connected.
  • Accessibility lets it type and click for you.
  • Local Network lets your iPhone find it on Wi-Fi.

Nothing is sent anywhere else. See the privacy policy.

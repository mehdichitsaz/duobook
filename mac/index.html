DuoBook Host for Mac

The free menu-bar app that lets DuoBook on your iPhone show your Mac’s screen and type and click on it.

Download for Mac Setup help

macOS 14 Sonoma or later · Apple silicon and Intel · Signed and notarized by Apple

Install

  1. Open the downloaded file and drag DuoBook Host to Applications.
  2. Open it from Applications. Its icon appears in the menu bar.
  3. Click Allow for Screen Recording and Accessibility when it asks; it opens the right page in System Settings.
  4. Turn on Open at login so it’s always ready.
  5. Want to use your Mac away from home? Turn on Access from anywhere.

Why it needs these permissions

  • Screen Recording sends your screen to your iPhone, only while it’s connected.
  • Accessibility lets it type and click for you.
  • Local Network lets your iPhone find it on Wi-Fi.

Nothing is sent anywhere else. See the privacy policy.
